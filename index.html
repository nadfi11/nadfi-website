About Us

Who we are

Curiosity can become change.

NADFI — Nature and Animal Development Federation Initiative — is a youth-led initiative built around learning, creativity, awareness, and care for the natural world.

We want to make nature and wildlife easier to understand and harder to ignore. Through creative projects, informative content, and small practical actions, we hope to inspire more people to care for the planet we share.

Small steps. Real care.

How can you help?

You don’t have to do everything. Start with one thoughtful action and help it grow.

Choose safe, practical ways to support nature. Respect wildlife from a distance, follow local rules, and work with trusted adults or established organisations for community activities.

Test your nature knowledge
01Reduce waste

Carry reusable items and sort waste where facilities are available.

02Respect wildlife

Never feed, chase, touch, or disturb wild animals. Observe from a distance.

03Share knowledge

Share reliable nature facts and check sources before reposting claims.

04Care for habitats

Join supervised clean-ups or planting projects using locally appropriate plants.

05Choose wisely

Avoid products made from threatened wildlife and support responsible choices.

06Create for change

Make a poster, write an article, or create art that inspires care for nature.

Want to contribute to NADFI? Prepare your idea, article, artwork, or volunteering suggestion and share it with the team through a contact method approved by a trusted adult. Avoid publishing personal phone numbers, home addresses, or private school details on this public site.
